THE LOVE STORY

From Clear Shampoo to Florence: The Virushka Fairytale

In 2013, Virat first encountered Bollywood actress Anushka Sharma on set for a Clear shampoo commercial shoot. As Kohli candidly confessed in an interview with Graham Bensinger, he was nervous and cracked a joke about her high heels to break the ice. Their relationship blossomed away from the media spotlight, leading to their intimate, dreamlike wedding on 11 December 2017 in Florence, Italy.

Florence Wedding (2017) Daughter Vamika (Born Jan 2021) Son Akaay (Born Feb 2024)

HEALTH TRANSFORMATION

The Vegetarian Shift for Peak Batting

In 2018, Kohli discovered that elevated uric acid levels were triggering a cervical spine condition that radiated pain to his fingers and hindered his batting grip. He eliminated meat completely, adopting a nutrient-dense vegetarian lifestyle that cured his condition and extended his athletic longevity.

SPIRITUALITY & INK

The Sacred Kara, Shiva & Cap Numbers

Kohli has worn a religious Sikh kara on his right wrist since 2012, alongside his white shoes superstition and single lucky pair of batting gloves. His tattoos carry profound meaning: Lord Shiva meditating on Mount Kailash, a Japanese samurai warrior, the sacred syllable Om, his parents' names (Prem & Saroj), and his India ODI (#175) and Test (#269) cap numbers.

GIVING BACK

The Virat Kohli Foundation

Transforming the lives of underprivileged children, nurturing future Olympians, and championing stray animal welfare.

🎓

Underprivileged Youth & Education

Established in 2013, VKF partners with Smile Foundation, Save the Children, and eBay to fund education, healthcare, and meal programs for thousands of underprivileged children across India.

🏆

Athlete Development Programme (ADP)

Identifies and funds promising young Indian athletes across non-cricketing sports—covering international coaching, nutrition, and gear. Supported athletes include champion golfer Aadil Bedi.

🐕

Animal Rehabilitation & Trauma Centre

Inspired by Anushka's love for animals, VKF collaborated with the Awaaz Foundation in Mumbai to establish two emergency care centers and a trauma facility for stray animals.

🤝

#InThisTogether COVID-19 Relief

Donated ₹3+ crore to PM CARES and state relief funds; spearheading a national Ketto fundraiser '#InThisTogether' that generated over ₹11 crore for life-saving hospital oxygen concentrators.
